# Learning to Prove

**Learning to Prove** is a research initiative at the Federal University of ABC (UFABC), Brazil, dedicated to automated reasoning and the integration of machine learning techniques into formal methods.

Our research explores the development of automated theorem provers, machine learning-guided proof search, and computational methods for logical reasoning and formal verification.

## Research Areas

- **Automated Theorem Proving:** Design and implementation of proof systems and automated reasoning algorithms.
- **Machine Learning for Reasoning:** Learning-based strategies for guiding proof search and improving automated theorem provers.
- **Formal Verification:** Logical and computational methods for analyzing and verifying computational systems.
- **Logic and Computational Methods:** Algorithms, representations, and tools for reasoning in classical and non-classical logics.
